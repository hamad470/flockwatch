# Model card: FlockWatch poultry sound classifier v1.0.0

## Intended use
This model classifies 5-second poultry-house audio clips as **healthy**, **unhealthy** (respiratory sounds such as coughs, sneezes and rales) or **noise** (no bird sounds). Its outputs feed a *flock-level* early-warning index.

It's a **decision-support tool for farmers and vets**. It doesn't diagnose disease, and it doesn't replace clinical examination or statutory reporting of notifiable diseases such as avian influenza.

## Model
- **Architecture:** compact CNN, 3 convolutional blocks (16/32/64 channels), global average pooling and a linear head. About 70k parameters.
- **Input:** a 64-band log-mel spectrogram of a 5 s clip at 16 kHz (n_fft 1024, hop 512), standardised per clip.
- **Training:**
  - AdamW optimiser with a cosine learning-rate schedule;
  - class-weighted cross-entropy;
  - SpecAugment-style time and frequency masking plus time shift;
  - early stopping on validation macro-F1 (patience 6).

## Data
- **Version 1.0.0** was trained on 1,200 synthetic clips (400 per class) from 120 recording sessions, generated by `src/flockwatch/audio_synth.py`.
- **Split:** by recording session, using group shuffle split. Train 830, validation 190, test 180. No session appears in more than one split.
- **For real use,** retrain on the public CC BY 4.0 datasets listed in the README, and then on recordings from the target farms.

## Performance (held-out test set)
| Class | Precision | Recall | F1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| healthy | 0.85 | 0.94 | 0.90 |
| unhealthy | 0.98 | 0.85 | 0.91 |
| noise | 0.96 | 1.00 | 0.98 |
| **Macro** | | | **0.93** (accuracy 93.3%) |

The most common error is a mild unhealthy clip being heard as healthy: 8 of 60 unhealthy test clips. These missed detections are mitigated at flock level by averaging many clips per day and by corroborating with water intake.

## Limitations
- Synthetic training data can't capture every real-world variation: breed, age, disease type, house acoustics and microphone placement.
- Performance on real recordings is unknown until retrained and validated.
- Probabilities are not calibrated. Alert thresholds should be tuned per farm using historical data.

## Ethical considerations
- **Animal welfare:** a missed detection delays care, and a false alarm wastes vet time. Thresholds should be set together with vets.
- **People on farm:** microphones may record staff speech. See `DATA_GOVERNANCE.md` for minimisation and deletion rules.
- **Accountability:** alerts are reviewed by a person. The model never triggers treatment automatically.
